Entry: one satirical sentence roasting lawmakers who regulate AI but
cannot convert a file to PDF, written by `main.ts` to `/output/entry.txt`.

## Final line (chosen)

> The senator who spent a year writing the law to govern artificial intelligence still calls his bill FINAL_final_v2, because the Export to PDF button is where his authority ends.

177 characters, one sentence.

## Why this line (judge criteria, in order)

1. On subject: a lawmaker regulating AI, and the file he cannot convert.
2. The turn: a year of lawmaking authority undercut by the Export to PDF
   button, where his authority ends.
3. Aim stays on power and competence: the bill, the law, the authority.
4. Reads clean in one pass, punch lands on the last few words.
5. Specific detail: FINAL_final_v2 is a file-naming habit everyone
   recognises, not a worn tech-illiteracy cliche.

MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=<dir> node main.ts
